The Founder's Journey

"Beauty is not just about looks, it's about feeling confident in your own skin."

Starting as a passionate stylist with a dream, our founder embarked on a journey to redefine luxury grooming in India. With years of international training and an eye for perfection, the first Ashrafs Salon was born out of a desire to provide world-class services locally.

Today, that singular vision has blossomed into a chain of premium salons across Pune and Mumbai, celebrated for unmatched expertise, bespoke treatments, and a relentlessly client-first approach.
